evcc is an extensible EV charge controller and home energy management system. It connects your EV charger, solar inverter, home battery, and energy meters to intelligently charge your vehicle using clean, solar-generated power — saving money and reducing your carbon footprint.

## Why evcc?

evcc runs entirely on your local network — no cloud account required, no subscription fees for core features. Your energy data stays at home.

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install evcc">
    Run evcc on Linux, macOS, Raspberry Pi, or Docker. See [Installation](/installation) for all methods.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your devices">
    Edit `evcc.yaml` to connect your energy meters, EV charger, and vehicles. Use the [Configuration guide](/configuration/overview) for reference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up charging modes">
    Choose between **Off**, **Now** (fast charge), **Min+PV** (minimum + solar surplus), and **PV** (solar only) modes. Learn more in [Charging modes](/features/charging-modes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automate and integrate">
    Connect evcc to Home Assistant, InfluxDB, or your own systems using the [REST API](/integrations/rest-api) or [MQTT](/integrations/mqtt).
  </Step>
</Steps>
